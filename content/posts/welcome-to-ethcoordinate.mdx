We’re excited to introduce EthCoordinate’s website: a home for anyone interested in understanding, participating in, and contributing to Ethereum coordination.

## What is EthCoordinate?

EthCoordinate is an independent Ethereum organization focused on coordination infrastructure. Like our octopus mark with its many arms, our work reaches across several connected areas of the ecosystem:

- **Protocol coordination**: We facilitate public processes around core development and adjacent efforts
- **Staker support**: We maintain trusted guidance, community spaces, and tooling for independent stakers
- **Forkcast**: We steward a clear view of upgrades, EIPs, calls, and implementation status
- **Protocol economics**: We support research and rigorous debate about Ethereum’s long-term economic health
- **Open-source tooling**: We maintain tools and documentation used by participants in Ethereum’s consensus set

## Why This Website?

Ethereum’s coordination landscape contains a wealth of information, but it can be intimidating for newcomers. This website is a friendly entry point that helps you:

1. Find the right initiative or support path
2. Understand how Ethereum governance works
3. Discover ways to contribute
4. Stay current on protocol development
